Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

ForJob.in is a jobs and talent platform backed by Expedite HRS. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and manage personal data when you visit ForJob.in, contact us, apply for a job, submit a hiring requirement, or use a form or service linked from this website.

Who is responsible for your data

ForJob.in and Expedite HRS are responsible for personal data collected through this website and its linked forms for the purposes described below. For privacy questions, correction requests, consent withdrawal, or complaints, email info@expeditehrs.com.

Personal data we may collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number, city, and professional profile links.
  • Job application information, CV/resume, work experience, skills, education, notice period, preferred location, and job preferences.
  • Employer contact details, company details, job requirements, salary range, hiring volume, and hiring timeline.
  • Messages, enquiries, feedback, and other information you choose to provide.
  • Basic technical information generated when you visit the site, such as browser/device information and page interactions, where provided by Blogger, Google, Cloudflare, analytics tools, or embedded services.

Why we use personal data

  • To publish and manage job opportunities.
  • To review applications and connect candidates with relevant roles or employers.
  • To review employer hiring requirements and provide recruitment-related services.
  • To communicate about your enquiry, application, registration, or service request.
  • To operate, secure, improve, and troubleshoot ForJob.in and its linked services.
  • To meet legal, regulatory, fraud-prevention, and record-keeping requirements.
  • To send marketing or job-related updates only where you have separately opted in or where otherwise permitted by applicable law.

Sharing of personal data

We may share relevant information with prospective or current employers, recruitment partners, and service providers only when necessary for the stated purpose, such as reviewing a job application or managing a hiring requirement. We may also use service providers that host or process data, including Blogger, Google services, Cloudflare, Tally, Google Sheets, and email providers.

We do not sell personal data. We do not publish a candidate’s personal contact information, CV, salary details, or other sensitive professional information on a public page without clear, separate consent.

Consent, correction, and withdrawal

Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time. You may also request access to, correction of, updating of, or deletion of your personal data, subject to legal and legitimate business retention requirements. To make a request, email info@expeditehrs.com with the subject:

Privacy request – ForJob.in – [Your Name]

We may request reasonable information to verify your identity before acting on a request. Withdrawal of consent will not affect processing already carried out before withdrawal, and may affect our ability to provide a requested service.

Data retention and security

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for recruitment, communication, legal obligations, dispute management, and legitimate record keeping. We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect data, but no internet-based system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Third-party websites and services

This site may link to third-party websites, job application tools, social-media platforms, or forms. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. Please review them before submitting information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, data practices, or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

Contact and grievance

For privacy enquiries, requests, or complaints, contact: info@expeditehrs.com.
