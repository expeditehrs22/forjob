ForJob.in is a jobs and talent platform backed by Expedite HRS. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and manage your job application information when you visit ForJob.in, contact us, apply for a job, submit a hiring requirement, or use a form or service linked from this website.

Who is responsible for your data

ForJob.in and Expedite HRS are responsible for the information collected through this website and its linked forms for the purposes described below. For privacy questions, correction requests, consent withdrawal, or complaints, email connect@expeditehrs.in.

Job application information data we may collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number, city, and professional profile links.
  • Job application information, CV/resume, work experience, skills, education, notice period, preferred location, and job preferences.
  • Employer contact details, company details, job requirements, salary range, hiring volume, and hiring timeline.
  • Messages, enquiries, feedback, and other information you choose to provide.

Why we use job application information

  • To publish and manage job opportunities.
  • To review applications and connect candidates with relevant roles or employers.
  • To review employer hiring requirements and provide recruitment-related services.
  • To communicate about your enquiry, application, registration, or service request.
  • To operate, secure, improve, and troubleshoot ForJob.in and its linked services.
  • To meet regulatory, fraud-prevention, and record-keeping requirements.
  • To send marketing or job-related updates only where you have separately opted in or where otherwise permitted.

Sharing of job application information

We may share relevant information with prospective or current employers, recruitment partners, and service providers only when necessary for the stated purpose, such as reviewing a job application or managing a hiring requirement.

We do not publish a candidate’s contact information, CV, salary details, or other sensitive professional information on a public page without clear, separate consent.

Consent, correction, and withdrawal

Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time. You may also request access to, correction of, updating of, or deletion of your job application information, subject to legal and legitimate business retention requirements. To make a request, email connect@expeditehrs.in with the subject:

Privacy request – ForJob.in – [Your Name]

We may request reasonable information to verify your identity before acting on a request. Withdrawal of consent will not affect processing already carried out before withdrawal, and may affect our ability to provide a requested service.

Data retention and security

We retain job application information only for as long as reasonably necessary for recruitment, communication, obligations, dispute management, and legitimate record keeping. We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information.

Third-party websites and services

This site may link to third-party websites, job application tools, social-media platforms, or forms. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, data practices, or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

Contact and grievance

For privacy enquiries, requests, or complaints, contact: connect@expeditehrs.in
